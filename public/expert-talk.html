A weekly platform built for trust

Expert Talk with Edgar is a weekly Horizon TV prime-time program featuring trusted experts in healthcare, law, business, and community services.

  • Trusted guests from healthcare, law, business, and community services
  • Real-world issues affecting Armenians in Los Angeles.
  • Stronger professional and cultural connections between Armenia and the diaspora.

Hosted by Edgar Ayvazyan, Executive Producer, Media & PR Strategist, and a producer of high-profile Armenian-American medical magazines, awards, and entertainment productions.

Full episodes remain available uncut on Horizon TV's YouTube channel.
